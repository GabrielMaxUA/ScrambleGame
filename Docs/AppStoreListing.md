**Description** (1712 / 4,000):
```text
LearnScrumble teaches you the words you actually use at work, in the language of your workplace.

Pick your job — cook, warehouse worker, electrician, cleaner, caregiver and 27 more — and the language you speak. Every word comes with a clear picture of the real thing, so you learn what it is, not just how it's written.

SPELL IT
Look at the picture and tap the scrambled letters into the right order. Wrong letter? Tap it to send it back. Stuck? The lightbulb shows the word in your own language, and the speaker says it out loud.

SAY IT
The pronunciation test listens while you say a word and tells you if it's right. You get three tries.

HEAR IT
The listening test plays a word and shows four pictures. Tap the right one. Use "Test all" to go through every word in a row.

YOUR PROGRESS
See how many words you've learned, your right and wrong answers, and your days in a row. "My words" keeps every word you've played, hardest first, and lets you repeat them — even without internet. A daily reminder helps you keep going, only on days you haven't played yet.

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
The app works in your own language — more than 50 languages, including right-to-left ones — while you learn the language of your job.

FAIR PRICING
Your first 10 words are free. Then unlock a profession once for its full word list, "My words" and the choice of voice. One-time purchase, no subscription, no ads.

Words and pictures are created with AI. If something looks wrong, tap the flag to report it — we check every report and fix it for everyone.

Privacy: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your progress stays on your iPhone.
Privacy Policy: https://learnscrumble.com/privacy
Terms of Use: https://learnscrumble.com/terms
```
