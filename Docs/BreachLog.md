# Breach log (registre des incidents de confidentialité)

**Organization:** Max Gabriel, sole proprietor operating as PawIT · **App:** LearnScrumble
**Kept by:** Max Gabriel, person in charge of the protection of personal information · **Started:** 1 November 2026

Required by PIPEDA (s. 10.3, keep every breach of security safeguards for **24 months**) and Québec's private-sector
privacy act (s. 3.8, a register of every confidentiality incident, given to the Commission d'accès à l'information on
request). Record **every** incident here, even ones that turn out harmless. Internal document — not published.

## When something happens

1. **Contain it:** rotate the affected key (OpenAI key in Secret Manager, Firebase service accounts), close the rule or
   bucket, revoke access.
2. **Assess the risk of serious / significant harm:** how sensitive the data is, how many people, whether it can be
   misused (LearnScrumble's server data is low-sensitivity and not linked to names).
3. **If there is a real risk of significant harm:** notify, as soon as possible,
   - the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada (priv.gc.ca → Report a privacy breach),
   - for Québec residents, the Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.gouv.qc.ca → incident de confidentialité),
   - the affected people (in the app or on the website if they can't be reached individually),
   - and any organization that could reduce the harm (e.g. Google, OpenAI).
4. **Record it below** the same day, then update it.
