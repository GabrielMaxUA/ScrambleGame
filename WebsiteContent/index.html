Feel at home at work.

Build the vocabulary. Gain the confidence. Unlock new opportunities.

LearnScrumble is a word game for people working in a new language. Pick your job and the language you need, and spell the words you'll actually use — with a picture, the sound of the word and a hint in your own language.

How to play

How it works

💼

Words from your job

Choose your profession — cook, warehouse worker, electrician and 29 more — 32 in all. The words come from that workplace.

🔤

Spell it, letter by letter

Look at the picture and tap the scrambled letters into the right order. Wrong letter? Tap it to send it back.

🎙️

Hear it, then say it

See the word in your own language and hear how it sounds. Then say it yourself in the pronunciation test — the app listens and checks it.

🌍

Your language, theirs

The app speaks your language — including right-to-left languages — while you learn the language of your workplace.

⏰

Progress, My words and a reminder

See how many words you've learned and how well you say them, repeat every word you've played in My words — even without internet — and get one reminder a day, only on days you haven't played yet.

🎁

Try 10 words free

Play your first 10 words for free. Then unlock your profession once — no subscription — to play the most-used workplace words of that profession, use My words and choose the voice.

Vocabulary, not safety training. LearnScrumble helps you learn the words used at work. It doesn't replace the safety training given at your workplace — always follow your employer's instructions.
