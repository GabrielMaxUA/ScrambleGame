Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DD Month YYYY]

In short: LearnScrumble has no account and doesn't ask for your name, email or phone number. Your settings and progress stay on your iPhone. To create new words and pictures, the app sends your job, your two languages and the words you've already practised to OpenAI, and shares new words and pictures in a common word library on Google Firebase — without anything that identifies you. We don't show ads, don't track you across apps and don't sell data.

2. Data stored on your iPhone

This data is created by you while using the app. It stays on your device and is not sent to us.

WhatDetailsWhy
Your setup The language you speak, the language you learn, your job (from the list or typed by you), speech speed, chosen voice per language, daily reminder on/off and time, whether you finished the setup and the "How to play" tutorial. To remember your choices between launches.
Learning progress For each word you've answered: the word's English name, the language you practised it in, how many times you were right and wrong, and when you last answered it. To show your scores, avoid repeating words, and build your review of difficult words.
Daily free-word counter Today's date, how many words you've answered today, and the latest date the app has seen. Kept in the iPhone's secure storage (Keychain). To apply the free limit of 20 words a day. It stays on your iPhone even after you delete the app, so the limit can't be reset by reinstalling.
Daily reminders The reminders planned for the next 7 days. They are scheduled by iOS on your iPhone; no server is involved. To remind you at your chosen time, only on days you haven't played.

You can delete your answers for one language with Settings → Progress → Reset progress, and all app data by deleting the app (except the daily free-word counter, see above). The data may also be included in your own iPhone backups.

Spoken words are produced by your iPhone's built-in text-to-speech, on the device.

3. Data sent to other services

OpenAI — creating words and pictures

When the app needs new words, it sends a request to OpenAI, L.L.C. (United States) with:

  • your job (as chosen or typed by you);
  • the language you speak and the language you learn;
  • the English names of words you've already practised in that language, so they aren't repeated;
  • for new pictures: a short description of the object to draw.

The app doesn't send your name or any account or device identifier. As with any internet request, OpenAI receives your IP address. Please don't type personal information (such as names) into the job field. According to OpenAI's policies, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default and may be kept for a limited time (currently up to 30 days) to monitor abuse.

Google Firebase — the shared word library

Words and pictures are stored in a library shared by all LearnScrumble users, on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), operated by Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd. The app:

  • reads words that already exist (by their English name), so known words and pictures are reused instead of created again;
  • adds new words: the English name, the word in your two languages, and a link to the picture;
  • uploads new pictures (images of objects, never photos of you).

Nothing that identifies you, and not your job, is stored with these words. Google receives your IP address and technical information (such as the app and SDK version and device type) needed to deliver the service. Data location: [Firebase project region, e.g. europe-west].

Apple

  • Time check: before a game starts, the app asks apple.com for the current time (only the time, no page content), so the daily free words are counted correctly. Apple receives your IP address.
  • Purchases: subscriptions are bought, renewed and restored through Apple's App Store. Apple processes your payment; we don't receive your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Notifications: if you allow them, daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone; no notification server or push token is used.

5. Why we use data (legal bases)

  • To provide the app you asked for — creating words and pictures for your job and languages, remembering your settings and progress (performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To keep the free limit fair and the service working — the daily counter and the time check (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • To build the shared word library so words and pictures are reused for all learners (legitimate interests, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). It contains no personal data.
  • Reminders only if you allow notifications (consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — you can switch them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

6. How long data is kept

  • On your iPhone: until you reset your progress or delete the app. The daily free-word counter stays in the Keychain until the iPhone is erased.
  • Shared word library: for as long as the service runs; it contains no personal data.
  • OpenAI: according to OpenAI's API data policy (see above).
  • Emails to support: for as long as needed to answer you, then [e.g. deleted after 12 months].

If you are in the EU/EEA or the UK you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict processing of your personal data, to object to processing based on legitimate interests, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.
