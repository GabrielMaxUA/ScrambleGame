1. The service

LearnScrumble is a word game that helps you learn the vocabulary of your job in another language. You choose your own language, the language you want to learn and your profession; the app shows a picture and you spell the word from scrambled letters, with a hint in your language and spoken audio. New words and pictures are created with artificial intelligence and need an internet connection.

2. Not safety training

LearnScrumble teaches workplace vocabulary only. It does not teach safety rules or procedures and does not replace the safety training, instructions or supervision provided at your workplace. Always follow your employer's instructions and the safety rules that apply to your work. Do not rely on the app to understand warnings, emergency instructions or safety-critical communication.

3. AI-generated content

Words, translations and pictures are generated by artificial intelligence and shared in a common word library. They may be inaccurate, incomplete, unnatural for your region, or show an object differently from the one used at your workplace. We work to correct mistakes — tap the flag on a wrong picture, or tell us about a wrong word — but we don't guarantee that any content is correct. When you report a picture, you get a new AI-drawn picture on your device; after we check it, it may replace the picture for every learner.

4. Free words

  • You can try the app with 10 free words. They're given once, not every day. Deleting and reinstalling the app doesn't give them again.
  • We may change the number of free words or which features are free. Changes don't affect a profession you have already unlocked.

5. Unlocking a profession

  • After the free words, each profession is unlocked with a one-time purchase (currently US$9.99 or the equivalent in your currency, as shown in the app before you buy). Apple charges the price and any applicable taxes, and shows the total before you confirm. There is no subscription and nothing renews.
  • Unlocking a profession gives you, for that profession: its most-used workplace words, My words (every word you've played with your right and wrong answers, which you can repeat, also without internet), and the choice of voice.
  • A purchase is for one profession only. Another profession is a separate purchase.
  • A profession's word list has an end. It holds the common, everyday items of that job, chosen with AI; its size differs by profession (usually a few hundred words, never more than 500). The list may still grow while learners play, until no common items are left. When you've played every word of the list, the app tells you; you keep My words and can repeat them. New words need an internet connection and the services described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Fair use: to protect the service from misuse, at most 100 new pictures are drawn for one user per day. Words that already have a picture don't count, and normal learning stays far below this. If you reach it, the app gives you words that already have pictures, or asks you to try again later; the limit resets every day.
  • Purchases are made through Apple's App Store and charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its rules: reportaproblem.apple.com. Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected.
  • Use Settings → Purchases → Restore purchases to get your unlocked professions back on a new or reset device signed in with the same Apple Account.
